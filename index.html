Overview

Orbit Drop is a casual arcade game for Android. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

Depending on how you play and which services are enabled:

  • Google Play Games Services (optional / when signed in): display name, player ID, and leaderboard scores for the Voyage leaderboard.
  • Gameplay data stored on your device: local high scores, settings, and similar preferences.
  • Technical / diagnostic data: standard Android and Google Play information needed to run, update, and secure the app.

We do not ask you to create a Bando Labs account inside the game, and we do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information (such as precise location, contacts, or payment card numbers) through Orbit Drop.

How we use information

  • Provide core gameplay and local/online leaderboards
  • Sign you into Google Play Games when you choose to use that feature
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the app
  • Comply with Google Play and legal requirements

Ads

Ads are not enabled in the current launch build. If we add advertising later (for example Google AdMob), we will update this policy before turning ads on and disclose any advertising identifiers or related data use in Play Console Data safety.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by Google (Google Play, Play Games Services, and related platform services) under Google’s terms and policies when you use those features.

Data retention

Local game data stays on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Play Games leaderboard data is retained according to Google Play Games policies and our game configuration.

Children’s privacy

Orbit Drop is a casual arcade game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that violates applicable law or Google Play requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your choices

  • You can play without signing into Play Games (online names/scores will be limited).
  • You can revoke Play Games access from your Google account settings.
  • You can clear local data or uninstall the app at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The effective date above will be revised when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
