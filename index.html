Overview

Orbit 99 is a casual arcade game for Android. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

Depending on how you play and which services are enabled:

  • Google Play Games Services (optional / when signed in): display name, player ID, and leaderboard scores for the Voyage leaderboard.
  • Gameplay data stored on your device: local high scores, settings, and similar preferences.
  • Technical / diagnostic data: standard Android and Google Play information needed to run, update, and secure the app.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad activity: when ads are shown, Google AdMob and related Google advertising services may collect the advertising ID and data about ad impressions, clicks, and device/app information used to serve and measure ads.

We do not ask you to create a Band of Rabbits account inside the game, and we do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information (such as precise location, contacts, or payment card numbers) through Orbit 99.

How we use information

  • Provide core gameplay and local/online leaderboards
  • Sign you into Google Play Games when you choose to use that feature
  • Show banner ads and optional rewarded videos (continue refills and cosmetic ship unlocks)
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the app
  • Comply with Google Play and legal requirements

Ads

Orbit 99 uses Google AdMob for on-screen banner ads and optional rewarded video ads. Each run starts with a small number of free continues. When that stack is empty, you can refill continues by spending in-game gems or by watching a rewarded video, which grants a continue refill directly (not gems-then-spend). Refills are limited per run. A separate optional rewarded video can unlock a cosmetic ship skin. Ads use a maximum content rating of G (general audiences). We block alcohol, dating, gambling, and adult categories. Ads are not requested as child-directed. Google’s advertising products are described in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by Google (Google Play, Play Games Services, AdMob, and related platform services) under Google’s terms and policies when you use those features.

Data retention

Local game data stays on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Play Games leaderboard data is retained according to Google Play Games policies and our game configuration. Advertising data is retained according to Google AdMob / Google advertising policies.

Children’s privacy

Orbit 99 is a casual arcade game. The Play target audience is ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in a way that violates applicable law or Google Play requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your choices

  • You can play without signing into Play Games (online names/scores will be limited).
  • You can revoke Play Games access from your Google account settings.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings (Google / Ads).
  • You can clear local data or uninstall the app at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The effective date above will be revised when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
